# The parts, by job

Generated by `smartui.py components --write` from `assets/set.json` and each part's `meta.json` in the library. Every part is a library component with slot marks; the catalog lists only the ones a project confirmed.

## Say something with its evidence (answer)

| Part | Library component | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `AnswerSources` | `answer-sources` | A written answer in a few short paragraphs, each with small numbered chips that point to the sources listed under it. |
| `KeyFacts` | `key-facts` | The facts about one thing as label and value rows, with a short note where it helps and a status colour on values that are a status. |
| `SummaryCard` | `summary-card` | One term, plan or policy explained: what kind of thing it is, a short summary, up to four key points and other names it goes by. |
| `CitationList` | `citation-list` | Numbered sources on their own: title, publisher and date, a short excerpt where it helps, and a button to open each one. |

## Show numbers (data)

| Part | Library component | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `DataTable` | `data-table` | A plain table of up to 50 rows with a title and a summary line, sortable by any column and shown ten rows to a page. |
| `BarChart` | `bar-chart` | Six months of spend as bars, with the current month's forecast hatched on top, a dashed budget line and a tooltip on the bar you point at. |
| `LineChart` | `line-chart` | One or two values over time as lines on a light grid, with the highest and lowest marked and a button that shows the same numbers as a table. |
| `DonutBreakdown` | `donut-breakdown` | Spend split into four categories as a donut with the total in the middle, and a legend that lists each amount and share. |
| `KpiStrip` | `kpi-strip` | Four headline numbers in one band, each with a plain note under it, and a small bar where a share matters. |
| `ProgressStatus` | `progress-status` | Where something stands: a status line coloured by its tone, an optional progress bar, the steps done and still to come, and when it was last updated. |

## Help the person pick (choose)

| Part | Library component | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `Comparison` | `comparison` | Two to four options side by side: each with its name, price and a Choose button, then one row per attribute so the differences line up. |
| `ResultList` | `result-list` | Up to eight results as cards: a photo, the name, one meta line, an optional detail and badge, a price and one button each. |
| `ProductCard` | `product-card` | A card for one product: a photo, category, name, one-line description, rating, price with the old price struck out, an Add to bag button and a save toggle. |
| `QuickReplies` | `quick-replies` | An optional question and two to six short answers as chips, so the person can reply with one tap instead of typing. |

## Collect input or ask before acting (act)

| Part | Library component | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `FormFields` | `form-fields` | A short form of one to eight fields (text, number, choice, date or switch) with hints, checks as you leave a field, and Continue and Cancel buttons. |
| `SetupSteps` | `setup-steps` | A short list of first steps with progress: what is done, the one step to do now with its time and a way to skip, and what comes next. |
| `TimeSlots` | `time-slots` | Free times grouped by day, one button per time, with the time zone stated and a plain line for a day with nothing free. |
| `ApprovalPrompt` | `approval-prompt` | Asks the person before something that changes data happens: what will happen, the details that matter, a warning when it cannot be undone, and Cancel or the action itself. |

## Say what happened (confirm)

| Part | Library component | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `Receipt` | `receipt` | What just happened: a status line in its tone, what was done, a reference number, the lines that matter, a total, a note and up to two next steps. |
| `ErrorRetry` | `error-retry` | Says in plain words what failed and that nothing else broke, with an optional error code, a Try again button and one other way forward. |
| `EmptyState` | `empty-state` | What a list shows when there is nothing in it: a small drawing in the system's own colours, what it means, when that changes, and one thing to do. |

## Show a picture, a place, a file or code (media)

| Part | Library component | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `ImageCard` | `image-card` | One photo with a caption, an optional credit line and an optional button, for when the answer is a picture of something. |
| `LocationCard` | `location-card` | A place without a third-party map: its name and address over a drawn street pattern with a pin, how far it is, its opening hours and an Open in maps button. |
| `FileCard` | `file-card` | One file to download: an icon for its kind, the file name, its type and size, an optional date, Download and an optional Preview. |
| `CodeBlock` | `code-block` | A piece of code with its file name and language, a Copy button, and long lines that scroll sideways inside the box. |

## Parts made for one kind of product; offered only when the scope step picks them (domain)

| Part | Library component | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `FlightResults` | `flight-results` | A list of flight options: departure and arrival times and airports, the duration over a stops line, the airline as a drawn mark, the price, Best and Cheapest tags, and sort chips. |

Every part handles the states in `states.md`; its fixture is `fixture.json` beside the part in the library.

ROSS Index: the top trending open-source startups, every quarter

We publish top trending open-source by their GitHub repos’ star growth since 2020 and call it the Runa Open Source Startup (ROSS) Index

Runa Capital actively invests in open-source startups (like Nginx and MariaDB) and considers an active developer community instrumental for software businesses. We always look for promising tech companies with a fast-growing army of fans, track them on Github and open-source part of our findings as an index.

Runa Open Source Startup (ROSS) Index publishes the top open-source startups by the growth rate of GitHub stars at their repositories. Such an approach allows for highlighting those OSS products that were on top of developers’ minds during the past quarter or year. Our index is transparent, measurable and entirely focused on startups.

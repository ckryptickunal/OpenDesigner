## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · gap
No watchOS type roles. The scale is a modular web scale (display/headline/title/body/label). The HIG watchOS styles at xLarge (default for 44/45/49 mm) are Large Title 40, Title 1 38, Title 2 30, Title 3 20, Headline/Body 17, Caption 1 16, Caption 2 15, Footnote 1 14, Footnote 2 13 (minimum 12). Even with raw.baseSize 17 the engine steps 14,17,21,27,33,42 here, so Title 3, Title 2, Large Title and Caption 2 had to be hand-set. Before baseSize was set, --audience large plus density 45-60 produced 11 and 14 px steps, below the watch floor.

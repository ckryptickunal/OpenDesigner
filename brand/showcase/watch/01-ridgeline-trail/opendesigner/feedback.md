## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · gap
Watch is not a real platform: sketch --platforms watch is accepted silently (help lists web,ios,android,desktop,secondary) and Q-plat-01/02/04 = watch change zero tokens ('recorded; the tokens already match this answer'). levers.json has platformBase 'watchos': 16 but the engine id is 'watch', so the native body size never applies (and only below brandPresence 50). A watch should imply dark-only + pure black, the watchOS text styles, a 44 pt target floor that the (pointer: fine) query cannot lower, and a corner safe area.

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · bug
Dark-only systems never show the exact brand colour: brandExact pins the hex only in the light ramp; the dark ramp lowers chroma (DARK_ACCENT_CHROMA 26/32), so #C8FF2E becomes accent-dark-9 #b0d468, #FF5436 becomes #e86d55, #FFCC1A becomes #e0c16c. bg-action-primary and bg-brand in a dark-only system point at the dusty step. Only --ds-color-brand-seed carries the real hex. With Q-theme-01 dark-only, pin the brand in the dark ramp.

## F-003 · 2026-10-14 · bug
sketch --delegated all replaced the supplied brand hex #C8FF2E with #617f00 ('a readable shade') because it assumed white text on the brand; with the brand not delegated the same hex was kept exactly with dark text. Delegating the brand should never change a hex the person typed; and on a dark-only product the bright neon is the readable one.

## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · gap
No presentation or mockup scene concept. A case-study scene (devices floating over a coloured field) needs a field colour, device geometry (bezel, corner radius, camera), materials (metal, glass) and long floating shadows. None of these are tokens; the elevation scale stops at 0 32px 64px, too short for an object floating above a surface. engine.py review then lists every device colour as a token bypass (17 in this scene, 30 in 02, 29 in 03, 24 in 07).

## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · idea
Placing a screen into a photographed laptop needs the display's active area, which is invisible when the screen is off. I fitted the lid's glass edges on the 4000 px original, solved the focal length from the two vanishing points (f = 6638 px) and the glass aspect from it (1.573, close to the laptop's spec), then inset a 16:10 area. A small helper that takes four clicked corners and a target aspect would save an hour per mockup and stop guessed perspectives.

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · idea
A mockup or presentation helper would save most of this scene's work: generic device frames (phone, laptop, tablet, watch, browser) drawn from tokens, a 3D stage that takes a photo's vanishing point, a homography helper that maps a render onto four measured corners of a board or screen, and shadow presets for floating and resting objects. Today each builder hand-writes the geometry and the maths.

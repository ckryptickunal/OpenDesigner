## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · bug · area color

Same mechanism changed #e8590c, #ff3d81 and #5fae7d in the other pieces.

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area color
Colorfulness mixes two things: how many hues and how saturated the one accent is. A monochrome phosphor brand (one hue, full chroma) is impossible: colorfulness 22 turned #ffb000 into a brown #a67d3e; colorfulness 85 restored chroma but also added accent2/accent3 ramps (172 token changes) until I set raw.accentCount 1.

Who may dredge the flats?

· started yesterday

How does Keller define who owns the tide flats, and do the 2019 interviews agree?

Careful reading· 2 sources · 41 seconds

Keller treats the estuary as a common pool held by custom rather than by title1. Anyone who has worked the flats for a full season may dredge them, and the right lapses when they stop. The 2019 interviews mostly agree, but four of the eleven fishers describe the flats as “nobody’s until you work them”2, closer to first possession than to Keller’s commons.

The harbour board’s minutes could settle it. Keller cites them once, for 1934, but never says who the board let dredge1.

Sources: Tidal Commons, Keller 1987, pp. 14 and 112 · Saltings interviews, 2019, #0412 and 3 others

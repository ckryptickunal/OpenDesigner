## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · bug · area color
Monochrome brand (#1A1A1A, colorfulness 0): accent-light-11 and accent-light-12 are both #000000 and accent-light-10 is #030303, so three ramp steps collapse into one. For a near-black brand the accent ramp should become the ink end of the neutral ramp, not duplicate black.

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area color, question Q-color-10
Alpha neutrals exist only as ink over the surface (neutral-alpha-light-*) and white over dark (neutral-alpha-dark-*). There is no paper-white alpha for a light veil over content in light mode (a palette's backdrop, a frosted sheet), so the veil is a raw rgb(253 253 253 / .72).

## F-004 · 2026-10-14 · idea · area review
review cannot tell a component's CSS from the layout of the page around it: absolute coordinates of a showcase or marketing layout (left: 48px, top: 80px) are reported next to real component values, and widths and heights are mapped to space tokens. A 'layout' kind, or a way to mark a region as page layout, would make the list readable.

## F-004 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area elevation, question Q-depth-01
No hard offset shadow. Neo-brutalism (a solid ink shadow with 0 blur that the element slams into on press) is a common style; Q-depth-01 offers shadow, tonal, borders, ring-shadow and glass, all blurred or none. A 'hard' depth model (offset steps, ink colour, pressed = 0) would cover it.

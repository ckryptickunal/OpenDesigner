## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · confusing · area motion
A brief asked for 'springy' toasts, but STD-components-toasts-drawers-07 locks the toast entrance to 400ms ease and STD-springs-gestures-04 holds damping at 1.0, so every spatial spring token is critically damped (no overshoot); only motion.spring.momentum overshoots (1.5%). Say in DESIGN.md where personality may add spring (stack fan-out, swipe snap-back) and where the locked recipe wins, so a builder does not have to work it out.

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · bug · area review
review flags a spinner's reduced-motion rotation (animation-duration: 1.6s) as 'UI animation over the 300ms budget' and, on the toast page, suggests replacing it with motion.duration.toast (400ms). A spinner's period is not a transition; constant motion is exempt from the budget (STD-easing-duration-01 already calls it 'constant motion').

## F-003 · 2026-10-14 · idea · area components
The toast standards (07 to 18) are a full component recipe: enter, stack lift and scale, shared height, hover fan-out with gap fill, 3 visible, 4 s with action toasts exempt, pause on hover and hidden tab, swipe. Only gap, visible, duration and offsets became tokens. Lift-per-index (gap) and scale-per-index (0.05) and the swipe threshold could be tokens too, so the stack can be built from tokens alone.

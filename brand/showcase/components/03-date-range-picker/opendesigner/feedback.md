## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · bug · area typography, question Q-type-08
With surface 'booking:operate' and expression 70 the engine chose a 14px base and ratio 1.333, so text.body-sm and label-sm came out 11px. Stage 11 says avoid anything people must read below 12px on web, and preflight errors under 12px (M8). Clamp the small steps to 12 on web, or raise the base. Fixed by answering Q-type-08 16 and Q-type-09 1.25.

## F-003 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area color, question Q-color-19
A price heat inside a calendar is a sequential scale, but the only sequential ramp is color.chart.sequential, which appears only after answering the charts question (Q-color-19), and is built from the accent hue: its step 4 is the same terracotta as the selected range and the primary button. In-UI heat (availability, prices, load) needs a sequential ramp that cannot collide with the selection colour.

## F-004 · 2026-10-14 · bug · area preflight
preflight.py html: (1) Google Fonts loaded with <link> (allowed by the showcase brief) are reported as 'used but has no @font-face' whenever a longhand font-family is written, but never when only the font shorthand is used, so the same page passes or fails by syntax; (2) in 'font: 800 var(--size)/24px var(--family)' the 24px line height is read as the font size; (3) token sizes in rem are invisible, so 'font sizes in px' is empty on token-built pages.

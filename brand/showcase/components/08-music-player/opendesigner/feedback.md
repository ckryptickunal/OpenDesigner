Gaps, bugs, confusing steps and ideas found while using OpenDesigner. Each entry has a ready-to-file issue link. Nothing is posted unless you open the link and submit it yourself.

## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area motion
Energy barely changes motion: at energy 82 (and at 20 in another system) the spring tokens, standard easing and medium/long durations are identical because house standards lock them; only motion.duration.extra and the heading weight move. DESIGN.md still says energy sets the motion. An expressive brand gets no bounce or overshoot anywhere, not even for a play-button pop, without overriding a standard.

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area color
No support for content-derived color (accent sampled from album art, a book cover, a photo). Q-color-06 covers wallpaper/device accent only. A player needs a slot for runtime art colors plus a rule for keeping text contrast on them (darken toward a base, scrim), which I had to invent.

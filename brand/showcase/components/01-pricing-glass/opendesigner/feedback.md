## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · bug · area color, question Q-color-01
sketch --brand '#F2A93B' silently replaced the brand with #a16900 (a darker brown) so white text passes, and stored the brown as raw.brandColor. Stage 08 says a light brand color should switch its button text to dark instead. A light amber, yellow or lime brand is lost at sketch. Workaround: set raw.brandColor back by hand; then dark accent-9 became #e8ad5e with dark text, which is right.

## F-003 · 2026-10-14 · idea · area imagery
Recolor maps a mono line drawing to neutral-1 / neutral-12 only, so a pastel product gets black-and-white art. The CSS-variable output made it easy to override --od-illus-neutral-1 per card with a pastel ramp step (that worked very well); a 'fill with accentN step 3' option in recolor would make this a documented path. 'check' also reports a leftover #000000 in the applied SVG.

## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · bug · area typography
The fallback stack for any named face is the system sans stack, even for a serif or Mincho face: font.family.text became '"Shippori Mincho", system-ui, -apple-system, ... sans-serif'. With raw.scripts including Jpan there are no Japanese fallbacks either (Hiragino Mincho ProN, Yu Mincho, Noto Serif JP). The fallback should follow the face's class and the scripts.

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area typography
No support for vertical writing (writing-mode: vertical-rl) in the type system: no vertical text style, no tracking or punctuation guidance for tategaki. raw.scripts Jpan does add a :lang(ja) line-height scale, which helped.

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · confusing · area typography
--surfaces 'research site:read' turned off marketingSurfaces, which silently capped the type scale at 2.5x body (largest size 38px). A reading site still has a hero and a newsletter ask; nothing said the display sizes were gone. Fixed with set raw.marketingSurfaces true. The sketch summary should say 'largest text 38px, because your surfaces are read/operate'.

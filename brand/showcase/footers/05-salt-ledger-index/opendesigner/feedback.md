## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area color
raw.neutralBase '#efe6d4' (paper) only takes the hue and chroma; step 1 stays near-white (#fefdf9) with chroma capped at 0.008, so the paper colour itself never appears. Nothing says which step to use as a page background for a cream or paper look; I picked step 3 by eye. A 'page tone' answer (white, paper, cream) that sets surface.base would help print-like brands.

## F-003 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area typography
There is no token or answer for numeral style (old-style vs lining figures) or small caps, which carry most of an editorial page; font-variant-numeric and the tracked uppercase labels are hand-written. raw.numericStyles exists but only toggles tabular numbers.

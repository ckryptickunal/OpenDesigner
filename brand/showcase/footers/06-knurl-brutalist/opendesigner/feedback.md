## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area depth
There is no depth style for a hard offset shadow (zero blur, solid ink, 4 to 8px), the signature of neo-brutalism. Q-depth-01 offers shadow, tonal, border and glass, and stages/15-depth-borders-and-materials.detailed.md says 'One hard shadow looks dated', which is a taste call written as a rule. With depth 40 the engine made soft shadows I could not use; the hard shadow is hand-written.

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · idea · area color
The engine warned that the lime action colour on a white surface is 1.16:1. Right call, but the fix it offers (point the role at a darker step) would lose the brand; the real fix in this style is a 4px ink border on every control. A hint that a border also satisfies 1.4.11 would help.

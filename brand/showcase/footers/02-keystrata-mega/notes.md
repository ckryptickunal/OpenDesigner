# 02 Keystrata: enterprise mega footer

## Direction
Keystrata is a fictional cloud security company (workload protection, posture, identity) selling to security teams at banks, hospitals and governments. Its footer has to carry a lot (six link columns, status, certifications, disclosure, region and language) and still look calm and exact: the visual promise is "we are precise". The signature is a live status console (90 days of uptime bars, region latencies) where most companies put a plain "All systems normal" dot. Light, cool, graphite type, one deep teal.
Feel words: trustworthy, technical, precise.

## System (engine)
```
python3 $E --dir .../02-keystrata-mega/opendesigner sketch --name "Keystrata" --brand "#0b7a6f" --audience dense --platforms web \
  --feel trustworthy,technical,precise --theme system-light-dark --delegated feel,audience,platforms,theme,surfaces --surfaces "marketing site:persuade"
```
- Delegated everything except the brand, so the teal stayed exact (white text on it is 5.2:1).
- "precise" is not a known feel word and was left out.
- Dials: expression 30 (a utility map), brandPresence 40 (brand in the status line and one accent), density 72 (six columns plus three bands), energy 30 (hover only), roundness 25 (2 px controls, 8 px card), depth 10 (hairlines, no shadows), colorfulness 22 (neutral plus teal plus status colours), warmth 30 (cool, formal).
- Faces: `set raw.textFace '"IBM Plex Sans"'`, `set raw.monoFace '"IBM Plex Mono"'` (both SIL OFL). Body 14, labels 12, tagline 29, wordmark 24 from the scale.
- Build: no errors, 0 warnings.

## Build
- All colours, spacing, sizes, radii, focus and motion are tokens. `engine.py review` found 17 raw values; I moved 7 to tokens and kept 10 on purpose (6 to 12 px dot and icon sizes, the 1 px bar radius): `review` suggested spacing tokens for them, which is the wrong role.
- Outside the tokens, on purpose: hairlines use neutral step 4 instead of `--ds-color-border-subtle` (step 7), because 40 rules at step 7 look heavy. The uptime bars are generated by script (deterministic).
- Logo: an arch of five stones with the keystone in teal, drawn as SVG.
- Accessibility: links at least 28 px tall (legal links 32 px), buttons 48 px, picker is a real button with an aria-label, status bars have a text alternative, all text 4.5:1 or better (tertiary #5e6471 on #fcfdff is 5.8:1, the pill text 6.1:1).

## Renders
- `shot.png`: 1440 wide, full height (about 799 px), scale 2. `shot-mobile.png`: 390 wide.
- Round 1: the incident callout overlapped the console header rule; the status URL was uppercased; the logo (three stacked trapezoids) read as a funnel. Fixed spacing, set the URL in lowercase, redrew the logo as an arch with a keystone.
- Round 2 (mobile): the two buttons broke onto two lines inside a fixed height, and the console header squeezed. Stacked the buttons full width and the header vertically; moved the callout so it stays inside the card.

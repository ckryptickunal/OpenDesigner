## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · confusing · area typography
Naming a specific typeface is hard to discover. sketch has no --font option and `feel` does not mention faces. The question route (pick Q-type-01 open-custom, then Q-type-03 for the style) only chooses among six built-in open faces; the pick hint then says to use `set raw.textFace` for any other face, which takes a JSON-quoted name. I found `raw.fontClass` plus `raw.textFace "=openClass"` by reading engine.py. A sketch --font option, or one line in DESIGN.md's Typography section naming the command, would save every builder the search.

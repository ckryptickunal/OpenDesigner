## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · bug · area typography
OPEN_FONTS (engine.py) misses common SIL OFL faces: Fredoka, Archivo, Newsreader, Big Shoulders Display, Shippori Mincho and others. DESIGN.md then lists 'Licence risks: Fredoka: confirm the web licence' for a free font. Either a longer list or reading Google Fonts metadata would fix it; until then set raw.fontLicence by hand.

## F-003 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area color
For illustration the ramps have a gap: steps 1-7 are pale and low-chroma, steps 8-12 jump to full colour. A meadow needs saturated mid-tones (hills, tree shade); I used accent3 steps 4, 6, 9 and 11. An 'illustration' or 'art' palette (5 to 7 saturated tones per hue) would let art stay on tokens.

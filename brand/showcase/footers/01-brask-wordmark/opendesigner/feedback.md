## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · bug · area color
sketch --delegated all silently replaces the --brand hex with a darker 'readable shade' (#ff4d00 became #d63f00) and DESIGN.md then calls the orange 'a red'. The brand hex was given, only the other answers were delegated; a given hex should stay exact, or the output should say loudly that it changed and how to keep it (--delegated feel,audience,... without brand).

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · confusing · area color

## F-006 · 2026-10-14 · idea · area color
Warmth above about 60 tints every neutral toward hue 58 (brown): Brask at warmth 62 gave #151514 and #ebe8e6 under an orange brand, Tumbletree at 90 gave brown ink #282321 and grey #605551 on a sky-blue page. The owner's new colour rule forbids brown-black with orange and muddy mid-tones. The engine could warn when warmth is high in dark mode with a red or orange brand, or decouple 'warm voice' from 'warm neutrals'.

## F-007 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area typography
The engine emits 8 font-size steps (here 12, 16, 21, 28, 38, 51, 67, 90) and preflight only warns above 10 sizes, while the owner's rule is about 6 sizes on a web piece, named after Apple and Material roles. A 'web marketing' cap of 6 steps, or a lint in review that counts the sizes a page actually uses, would catch it (piece 06 used 8 before I trimmed it).

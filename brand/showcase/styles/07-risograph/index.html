The waiting room study

We spent 412 hours in 14 waiting rooms at three hospital groups in Denmark and Sweden, timing 1,860 waits from check-in to call-up. On the way out, 96 patients told us how long it had felt.

The gap was widest where nothing showed people their place in the queue. In 2026, six clinics piloted a live queue board and a text message ten minutes before each turn. The real wait barely moved. The felt wait fell by 24 minutes.

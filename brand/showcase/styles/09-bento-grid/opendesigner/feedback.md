## F-004 · 2026-10-14 · idea
Tabular figures on body text: in Schibsted Grotesk 'tnum' also widens . , : so 'keep.' renders 'keep .' and '14:32' as '14 : 32'. Typography guidance should say: tnum only on columns of numbers, never on body or single values.

## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · gap
Choosing a mono face for a terminal style needs a glyph-coverage check the engine never does: Martian Mono (OFL, Google Fonts) has no box-drawing (U+2500), block (U+2580) or arrow glyphs, so frames fall back to another face at a different advance width (24.08 vs 28.0 at 40px) and misalign. Of 24 open monos tested, Azeret, Space, Sometype, Reddit, Fragment, Inconsolata, Ubuntu Sans Mono, Cascadia and Atkinson Mono also miss them. A coverage list for raw.monoFace, or a validate warning, would have caught it (rackhound hit the same thing).

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · bug
preflight.py html reports M9 'font is used but has no @font-face' for every Google Fonts family loaded with a <link> (Lilex here; Schibsted Grotesk, Funnel, Onest, Silkscreen in 09-12). document.fonts shows each used weight 'loaded'. M9 should follow <link> stylesheets from fonts.googleapis.com or downgrade to a note.

## F-003 · 2026-10-14 · gap
A docs hero needed one display size above the dense scale's top (35 px): the headline sits at 40 px off-scale. For audience=dense the scale has no display role at all, but read-mode surfaces (docs) still need one hero size. Q-scope surfaces 'docs:read' did not add it.

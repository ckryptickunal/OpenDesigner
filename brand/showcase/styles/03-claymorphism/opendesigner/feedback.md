## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area elevation, question Q-dir-01
No clay (or skeuomorphic) option in Q-dir-01; tonal was the nearest. Claymorphism needs a three-layer elevation token (inner light top-left, inner shade bottom-right, soft cast shadow), a coloured-clay variant whose highlight is limited so white text keeps 4.5:1 (a 45% highlight dropped 'Add to calendar' to 4.31:1), and an inset 'dimple' for empty checkboxes. All hand-built in brand/showcase/styles/03-claymorphism/index.html.

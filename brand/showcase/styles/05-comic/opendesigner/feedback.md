## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · gap
Named styles (comic, Memphis, risograph, Bauhaus) have no answer in Q-dir-01: the six presets are flat2, tonal, glass, neo-brutalist, soft, maximal. For a comic I picked neo-brutalist for its thick borders, but its offset shadow is wrong for comics and I had to set depth 0 by hand. A 'named style' answer (or a style catalogue that maps a style to dials plus the traits dials cannot hold) is missing.

SEV-2  Postmortem · INC-4471 · Tuesday 6 October 2026

The case of the three‑second timeout

A rollback that fixed nothing, a config key nobody read, and the night 1 checkout call turned into 16. Written by Ilse Brandt, on call, and Kwame Mensah, incident lead.

Window
02:06–02:47 UTC · 41 min
Impact
4,180 of 33,712 card checkouts failed (12.4%)
Cause
Renamed config key: the ledger timeout fell from 30 s to a 3 s default, and the 02:00 settlement batch pushed latency past it
Status
Resolved · 3 of 3 actions shipped

The incident as a seven-panel comic. A text version follows each panel.

What we changed

Three actions, all shipped within three days. None of them is “be more careful”.

Action 1 · shipped 8 Oct

Unknown config keys now fail the deploy instead of being skipped.

Schema check in CI · owner: platform
Action 2 · shipped 9 Oct

Only the gateway retries, with jitter and a budget of 10% of calls.

payments-gw 4.19 · owner: payments
Action 3 · shipped 9 Oct

The ledger timeout is 10 s, set on purpose, with a page at p99 over 5 s.

ledger SLO v2 · owner: ledger

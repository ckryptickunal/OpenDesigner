## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area elevation, question Q-dir-01
Glassmorphism has no home: the glass preset is Apple's control material (surface.glass 72% opaque, 'controls and navigation only, never content'). The style puts content on clear glass and needs tokens for fill opacity, rim highlight, backdrop saturation, grain, and a per-pane text contrast rule over a variable backdrop. All set by hand in brand/showcase/styles/01-glassmorphism/index.html.

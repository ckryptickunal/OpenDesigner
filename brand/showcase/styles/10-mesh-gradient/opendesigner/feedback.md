## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · gap
No way to record a brand gradient. The mesh field (ground #1b1370, cyan baseline, violet candidate, lilac peaks, grain, blur) lives only in index.html. 'brand gradients' is listed as a designer hook, but there is no token group (stops, angle or field rule, grain amount, where it may appear, what text may sit on it) and validate cannot check text contrast over it; I measured it by hand (tick labels failed at 1.75:1).

## F-003 · 2026-10-14 · idea
Making a gradient 'deliberate' needed a rule the guidance does not give: draw it from the product's own data (here the run's case scores), label it like a figure (legend, axis, a marked cluster), and keep text on a solid strip. making.md section 11 says only 'a gradient must carry content'; a short recipe would help.

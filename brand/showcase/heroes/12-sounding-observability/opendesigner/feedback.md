## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · bug
Density modes do not apply on <html>: tokens.css puts the default block ':root, [data-density="compact"]' after '[data-density="comfortable"]' with equal specificity, so <html data-density="comfortable"> still gets compact sizes (control-lg rendered 40 px, not 48). It works only on a descendant such as <body>. Emit the default block first, or scope modes as ':root[data-density=...]'.

[File this as an issue](https://github.com/ckryptickunal/OpenDesigner/issues/new?title=%5Bbug%5D%20Density%20modes%20do%20not%20apply%20on%20%3Chtml%3E%3A%20tokens.css%20puts%20the%20default%20bloc...&body=%2A%2AKind%3A%2A%2A%20bug%0A%0A%2A%2AWhat%20happened%20or%20what%20is%20missing%3A%2A%2A%0ADensity%20modes%20do%20not%20apply%20on%20%3Chtml%3E%3A%20tokens.css%20puts%20the%20default%20block%20%27%3Aroot%2C%20%5Bdata-density%3D%22compact%22%5D%27%20after%20%27%5Bdata-density%3D%22comfortable%22%5D%27%20with%20equal%20specificity%2C%20so%20%3Chtml%20data-density%3D%22comfortable%22%3E%20still%20gets%20compact%20sizes%20%28control-lg%20rendered%2040%20px%2C%20not%2048%29.%20It%20works%20only%20on%20a%20descendant%20such%20as%20%3Cbody%3E.%20Emit%20the%20default%20block%20first%2C%20or%20scope%20modes%20as%20%27%3Aroot%5Bdata-density%3D...%5D%27.%0A%0A%2A%2AEngine%3A%2A%2A%201.0.0%20%28Python%203.10%29%0A%0A_Filed%20from%20%60engine.py%20feedback%60.%20Please%20remove%20anything%20private%20before%20submitting._&labels=bug%2Cfrom-engine)

A product's marketing hero should inherit the product's state (brand, faces, dials, overrides) and add a display set; I copied state.json, decisions.md and standards files by hand, then picked Q-brand-06 two-sets.

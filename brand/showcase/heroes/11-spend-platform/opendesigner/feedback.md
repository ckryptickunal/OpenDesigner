## F-006 · 2026-10-15 · idea
The brand colour is used for every role by default (primary button, links, selection, focus, progress). A finance product that wants the accent to mean one thing ('posts tonight') has to override five roles by hand. A 'single-job accent' option (actions and links in ink, accent reserved for one named state) would help B2B products.

## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area color
A very dark brand seed (espresso #3A2418) gives an accent ramp with no colour above step 8: steps 1-8 are #fdfdfc to #8c8581, plain greys, so there are no tints, no mid-tone browns and nothing to paint with. Dark seeds need the ramp's chroma held up in the light steps, or a separate tint ramp.

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · confusing · area density
The Density dial couples whitespace and type size. For an 'extreme whitespace' luxury page I set density 5 and the body text grew to 19 px and labels to 14 px, the opposite of fine, small couture type. Density 30 gave 16/12. Whitespace between blocks and the size of text are separate decisions; the dial's help should say it changes text size, or split it.

## F-003 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area color
High Warmth plus a warm brand silently produces the brown-black dark mode with orange accents that reads muddy (seen on piece 03: warmth 85, clay brand, dark ground #171512 with terracotta). validate passes because contrast is fine. A tone check (dark ground hue and chroma vs accent hue) or a warning above Warmth 70 would catch it.

## F-007 · 2026-10-15 · gap
A map or chart drawn as hero art has no tokens for its parts: graticule lines, contour lines, index contours, frame labels, scale bars. Each builder hand-mixes alphas and sizes, and labels end up under scrims at 1.35:1. A small 'figure' token set (line, line-strong, frame-label colour that clears 4.5:1 on the page ground, label backing) would make data art consistent.

## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · bug · area color

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area color
No way to set the lightness of the light page ground. surface.base is always step 1 (near white, #fefcfb even at warmth 70); raw.neutralBase only takes hue and chroma. Off-white paper grounds (Swiss posters, editorial, luxury) have to borrow bg.neutral.subtle (step 3), a chip fill role. A color.light.baseY override like color.dark.baseY would fix it.

[File this as an issue](https://github.com/ckryptickunal/OpenDesigner/issues/new?title=%5Bgap%5D%5Bcolor%5D%20No%20way%20to%20set%20the%20lightness%20of%20the%20light%20page%20ground.%20surface.base%20is%20...&body=%2A%2AKind%3A%2A%2A%20gap%0A%0A%2A%2AWhere%3A%2A%2A%20area%20color%0A%0A%2A%2AWhat%20happened%20or%20what%20is%20missing%3A%2A%2A%0ANo%20way%20to%20set%20the%20lightness%20of%20the%20light%20page%20ground.%20surface.base%20is%20always%20step%201%20%28near%20white%2C%20%23fefcfb%20even%20at%20warmth%2070%29%3B%20raw.neutralBase%20only%20takes%20hue%20and%20chroma.%20Off-white%20paper%20grounds%20%28Swiss%20posters%2C%20editorial%2C%20luxury%29%20have%20to%20borrow%20bg.neutral.subtle%20%28step%203%29%2C%20a%20chip%20fill%20role.%20A%20color.light.baseY%20override%20like%20color.dark.baseY%20would%20fix%20it.%0A%0A%2A%2AEngine%3A%2A%2A%201.0.0%20%28Python%203.10%29%0A%0A_Filed%20from%20%60engine.py%20feedback%60.%20Please%20remove%20anything%20private%20before%20submitting._&labels=gap%2Cfrom-engine)

## F-004 · 2026-10-14 · bug · area tokens
House standard STD-accessibility-motion-13 says web spacing must be rem or em, and engine.py review flags every px gap, but the generated tokens.css writes the whole space ladder in px (--ds-space-8: 8px). The system breaks its own standard; emit rem (0.5rem) or exempt token values.

## F-006 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area theme
A single-mode marketing hero cannot be sketched: --theme light-only or dark-only is refused by STD-visual-details-24 (light and dark for every color role). Poster-like brand pages (a dark-only dev tool hero, a yellow festival poster) are a common, legitimate case; the standard could scope to product UI surfaces (operate/read) and allow one mode for persuade/experience surfaces.

## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · bug · area tokens

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area elevation
No hard offset shadow style. Q-depth-03 tells the person to avoid single hard shadows ('one hard shadow looks dated'), and no option produces them, but zero-blur offset shadows are the defining device of neo-brutalist and retro-print brands. Worked around by overriding elevation.raised / elevation.floating with DTCG shadows (6px 6px 0 and 10px 10px 0 in neutral 12), which validated fine. Offer a 'hard offset' shadow option tied to high Expression and low Roundness.

## F-003 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area preflight
preflight.py html does not flag infinite CSS animations (a scrolling ticker) that have no pause control; WCAG 2.2.2 needs one for moving content that lasts over 5 s. The paint skill's ingest writes a pause control, but a hand-built marquee gets no warning.

## F-005 · 2026-10-14 · confusing · area audience
In sketch, --audience large reads like 'big type' for a poster page. It actually recorded the plain words 'people who use it now and then, often on a phone' (DESIGN.md), which nobody would guess from the word 'large'. The help text only lists dense|regular|large; one plain line per option would stop that misreading.

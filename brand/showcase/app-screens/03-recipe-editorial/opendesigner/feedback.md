## F-005 · 2026-10-14 · idea · area color, question Q-color-14
Q-color-14 'alternating' plus a warm raw.neutralBase is what made a cream paper page possible (base = step 2 #faf3e9, cards = step 1). Without it the base is near-white because ramp ends cap chroma. Worth suggesting when the feel is 'paper', 'print' or 'editorial'.

## F-006 · 2026-10-14 · gap · area color, question Q-color-09
Warmth 85 plus a warm raw.neutralBase gave brown secondary text (#67543b) and a brown-black inverse surface (#272420) under a red-orange accent; the owner's rule calls this muddy. Nothing in validate warns about it. Because the neutral chroma curve peaks at text steps (9-11) and is lowest at paper steps (1-2), 'cream paper with neutral ink' is the opposite of what the curve gives. A separate paper tint vs ink tint, or a warning when text steps exceed ~0.02 chroma, would help.

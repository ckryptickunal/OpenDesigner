## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · bug
opendesigner-review probe.js measures text contrast against the nearest ancestor background, not what is painted behind: white initials on a progress fill (a sibling span) and a white number over an SVG octagon were reported as 1.29:1 and 1:1. Both were readable; restructured the markup so the checker could see the real background.

## F-003 · 2026-10-15 · gap
Blind pairwise test (ROUND3-BRIEF round 4 rule 1): the Mobbin references for roadmap/Gantt screens are demo accounts with empty or placeholder data (emails as names, '0% of 0', 2 rows). Judges picked our side on content every time, while a strict fresh-eyes audit scored the same render 3/5. The test mostly measures data richness unless the references have real data. Suggest: say in making.md section 5 to pick references with populated data, and weight the fresh audit higher when references are sparse.

## F-004 · 2026-10-15 · bug
The warning role reads as brown: text.warning #845d00 on bg warning-2 #f8f4ed plus warning-9 #efb239 marks were called 'brown, orange and cream' by a fresh audit, the combination the owner bans. Neutral warmth 30 and a cool tint do not move the warning ramp. Switched the late state to the danger role. The status ramps need a cooler, more saturated amber option, or a warning text step that is not brown.

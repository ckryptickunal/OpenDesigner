## F-001 · 2026-10-14 · confusing
engine.py review suggests spacing tokens for element sizes (width: 20px -> var(--ds-space-20) on icons and avatars) and elevation for 1px inset rings, and says 'this system uses borders, not shadows' for an inset hairline (which is a border). Most of its 78 hits on this page are noise; size.icon and size.control tokens should be suggested for sizes.

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · bug
opendesigner-review probe.js measures text contrast against the nearest ancestor background, not what is painted behind: white initials on a progress fill (a sibling span) and a white number over an SVG octagon were reported as 1.29:1 and 1:1. Both were readable; restructured the markup so the checker could see the real background.

# 04 · Voltfield: live workout

## Direction
Voltfield is a fictional run-coaching app for people who train with intervals. This is the screen you glance at mid-stride: the heart rate has to be readable at arm's length while bouncing, the zone obvious in one color, and the plan for the next 300 m one line long. Black, acid lime, one hot pink for the top zone, giant condensed numerals, chamfered hard edges. Feel words: **energetic, bold, techy**.

Shape language: 0 px corners, chamfered (cut-corner) buttons, skewed segments and chips, a faint 39 px grid. Type: Archivo at 62-75% width for every number and heading, JetBrains Mono for telemetry labels.

## System (engine)
```

pick Q-color-01 keep-hex · set raw.brandColor "#D4FF3A" (restored from #677e00)
pick Q-color-04 contrasting · set raw.secondaryColors ["#FF2E63"] · pick Q-color-09 pure · pick Q-color-21 black
pick Q-depth-01 borders
pick Q-type-01 brand-everywhere · set raw.brandFace "Archivo" · set raw.monoFace "JetBrains Mono" · pick Q-type-02 yes · pick Q-type-08 17
set overrides.type.displayReach 10   (a 169 px step for the heart rate)
```
Dials: expression 90, brandPresence 85, density 55 (several live readings), energy 95 (heading weight 750, display 850), roundness 5 (0 px), colorfulness 85, warmth 20; depth 8 from Q-depth-01 `borders`.

Rendered in the dark mapping.

## Fonts
Archivo (variable width and weight) and JetBrains Mono, both SIL OFL, Google Fonts.

## Where I went outside the tokens, and why
- **The neon is `--ds-color-brand-seed`**, not `--ds-color-bg-brand`: in dark mode the accent ramp's step 9 is #b6d269, the lime with its chroma cut, so it would not glow. The seed token is still generated by the engine.
- Zone colors: Z1-Z3 are neutral steps 8, 11 and 12, Z4 the brand seed, Z5 `accent2` (pink). I first used neutral step 6 for Z1, which is 1.5:1 on black; zone fills now keep 3:1 or more.
- Neon glows (text-shadow, box-shadow with no offset) are marked `od-ignore`: light, not elevation.
- `font-stretch` per element (62-75%): the tokens have no width axis.
- The 169 px heart rate keeps tabular figures so it does not jitter as it changes; that leaves a visible gap after the "1".

## Checks
- `engine.py validate`: 0 errors, 2 warnings (light mode: the lime on white is 1.14:1, which is expected for a neon brand and irrelevant to this dark screen, but it shows light mode is not usable as generated).
- `engine.py review`: 18 findings before I marked the neon glows `od-ignore`, 13 after: element sizes, the home indicator (it suggests the 0 px container radius for a 3 px pill), and `#ffffff` used inside an SVG mask.
- `preflight.py html --medium web`: 2 errors (Archivo and JetBrains Mono "have no @font-face"; they load through `<link>`). It also warned about tracking -0.035em on the hero number and a `<br>` in the cue; I loosened the tracking to -0.025em and replaced the `<br>` with block spans.
- Contrast (computed): lime on black 18.16:1, black on lime 18.16:1, secondary #979797 on black 7.19:1, pink #e9556e on black 5.99:1.
- Targets: lock 44, lap 96 × 72, pause about 230 × 72.
- Four full-size rounds: (1) a dead 100 px gap above the controls, units colliding with column rules, the "-12 MIN" label hidden under the zone bar, and broken corners on the outlined buttons (clip-path cut the inset border); (2) rebuilt the outline as two clipped layers, moved units into the labels, added the coach cue; (3) the cue wrapped "90 / s." and ran to three lines, so I shortened it; (4) loosening the hero tracking pushed the zone chip to two lines; it now stays on one.

## Process findings
1. **Bug, third time: the sketch darkened the brand** `#D4FF3A` to `#677e00`.
2. **Bug: keep-hex pins only the light ramp.** A neon brand cannot be exact in dark mode, where it matters most.
3. **Confusing: review calls glows "shadows"** in a borders-only system.
4. **Gap (shared with 01 and 02): preflight has no app medium** and flags the Google Fonts faces the brief allows.
5. **Helped:** `displayReach` as an override gave a real 169 px step (and 127 px) inside the scale, so even the hero numeral is a token; `contrasting` plus one secondary hex gave a clean pink ramp for zone 5.

## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · bug · area color, question Q-color-01
keep-hex pins the brand hex only in the light ramp. In dark mode accent step 9 is #b6d269 (chroma cut by DARK_ACCENT_CHROMA), so a neon lime that should glow on black is greyed. bg.brand in dark also points at the dulled step. A neon or fluorescent brand needs the exact hex in dark mode; I used --ds-color-brand-seed directly.

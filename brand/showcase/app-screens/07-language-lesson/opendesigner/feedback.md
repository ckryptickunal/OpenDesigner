## F-002 · 2026-10-14 · gap
iOS body size depends on Brand presence: with brandPresence 85 the native 17 pt base is dropped and the web base gives body-md 14 px and labels 11 px on an iPhone app (16/12 with Q-scope-06 experience). Had to set raw.baseSize 17. Expressive consumer iOS apps should keep 17 pt body.

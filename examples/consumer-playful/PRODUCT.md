## Product

A habit tracker where friends cheer each other on.

## Audience

Casual users on phones, a few minutes a day, many first-time visitors.

## Surfaces

- Today feed: Experience mode (density spacious; hero moments and big imagery allowed; display styles welcome)
- Onboarding and store pages: Persuade mode (density spacious; one hero line per page; sections, not card grids)
- Settings: Operate mode (density set by the Density dial; no hero; cards only to group related data)

## Memorable Thing

The streak celebration.

## Principles

1. Celebrate progress, never shame a miss.
2. Big, friendly targets for thumbs.
3. One joyful moment per screen, not ten.

Tie-break: the higher-ranked principle wins.

- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA
- Platforms: ios, android, web

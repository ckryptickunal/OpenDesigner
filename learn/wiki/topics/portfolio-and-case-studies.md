# Portfolio and case studies

## Overview

How to fill a portfolio with fake projects, where to host it (Dribbble, Behance, Notion, personal site) and how many projects to include (2-3 case studies or 3-4 interfaces).

- [[sources/59XWYgN00nQ-create-a-portfolio-with-no-experience-or-clients-needed|Create A Portfolio With No Experience (or clients) Needed]]: How to fill a portfolio with fake projects, where to host it (Dribbble, Behance, Notion, personal site) and how many projects to include (2-3 case studies or 3-4 interfaces).
- [[sources/5JxUJ1fuyO8-make-one-design-change-to-actually-land-clients-stop-struggling|Make ONE Design Change to Actually Land Clients (Stop Struggling)]]: Showing the finished website is no longer enough; show short case studies that list services, define the problem and present the solution with numerical evidence.
- [[sources/6CC8lLnqa28-6-things-you-probably-need-to-hear-as-a-web-designer|6 Things You Probably Need to Hear (as a web designer)]]: Getting hired is competitive; do work you are proud of, align with what companies look for, and honestly ask whether you would hire yourself from your resume and portfolio.
- [[sources/7cTdCu8HMgM-the-definitive-process-to-present-uis-like-a-pro|The DEFINITIVE process to present UIs like a pro]]: The creative showcase techniques are described as best for portfolios and social media, with guidance on backgrounds, collages and when to use photos.
- [[sources/9WVt1CelBfg-the-stupid-simple-way-to-learn-ui-ux-design-in-exactly-10-minutes|The stupid simple way to learn UI/UX design in exactly 10 minutes]]: Starting a portfolio while still learning is called the biggest mistake, because taste will change over the next one to two months.
- [[sources/ADaQuZS04Rc-stop-making-pretty-uis-think-like-a-product-designer|Stop Making Pretty UIs. Think Like a Product Designer]]: A portfolio of products that solve a problem, with states and flows, makes landing a job more likely than polished visuals alone.

- [[sources/eeN7yUcIWbw-20-top-underrated-web-design-resources-for-2025|20 Top Underrated Web Design Resources for 2025]]: Wall of Portfolios shows each designer's experience and location, which helps put their work in perspective.

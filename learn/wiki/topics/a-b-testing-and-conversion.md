# A/B testing and conversion

## Overview

Conversion rate optimization is the better add-on for high-traffic sites; compare analytics before and after a redesign, because pre-launch UX tests often have samples too small to be significant.

## Source Mentions

- [[sources/5JxUJ1fuyO8-make-one-design-change-to-actually-land-clients-stop-struggling|Make ONE Design Change to Actually Land Clients (Stop Struggling)]]: Conversion rate optimization is the better add-on for high-traffic sites; compare analytics before and after a redesign, because pre-launch UX tests often have samples too small to be significant.
- [[sources/9ypqs_2fAl8-we-studied-2-995-paywalls-heres-what-actually-converts|We Studied 2,995 Paywalls. Here’s What Actually Converts.]]: Case results (Opal 7% to 17%, Slopes +25%, Outsider five times conversion, Headspace 14-day winner) and the conclusion that radically different design tests move the needle most; there is no universal best paywall.
- [[sources/ARq1bx3Sfg8-the-psychology-behind-streaks|The Psychology Behind Streaks]]: Reports Duolingo tests: up to two equipped streak freezes raised daily active learners by 0.38%, and a one-lesson rule beat the full-daily-goal rule with over 40% more people keeping a 7-day streak.
- [[sources/E7RzEZ8GlHE-he-tested-4-700-paywalls-these-won|He Tested 4,700 Paywalls. These Won.]]: Test order (design, then personalization, then price packaging, with device-based pricing last), running a control against four to five challengers, patience about statistical significance, and choosing metrics (ARPU, paywall percentage, trial to paid, LTV, D1, D7, D30, churn) by business type.
- [[sources/Qsq-Sj_rojU-i-studied-1-460-onboarding-flows-here-s-what-i-found|I Studied 1,460 Onboarding Flows. Here's What I Found.]]: Cites results: multi-goal selection +10% trial conversion, conversational quiz copy +5% subscriptions, tailored plans almost +20% upgrades, a checklist +10% relative retention, a split sign-up form +15% conversions.
- [[sources/qK7WYCMvjUw-he-spent-1m-on-a-b-tests-here-s-what-won|He Spent $1M on A/B Tests. Here's What Won.]]: Hundreds of experiments a month over six years moved paid-traffic conversion from 10% to 40%; pricing tests are called the most important; many winners contradicted common advice.

# Shape and corner radius

## Overview

Rounded corners and large bubbly buttons make a site feel friendly; much less rounded corners help it feel modern and professional.

## Source Mentions

- [[sources/9WVt1CelBfg-the-stupid-simple-way-to-learn-ui-ux-design-in-exactly-10-minutes|The stupid simple way to learn UI/UX design in exactly 10 minutes]]: Rounded corners and large bubbly buttons make a site feel friendly; much less rounded corners help it feel modern and professional.
- [[sources/AH_ugxmLeUM-7-ui-ux-mistakes-that-scream-youre-a-beginner|7 UI/UX mistakes that SCREAM you’re a beginner]]: Mixed corner radii make a design feel amateur; set all smaller components to one radius (10 pixels in the example).
- [[sources/c1TvOcKdBVE-the-8-ui-ux-cheat-codes-for-instantly-better-designs|The 8 UI/UX Cheat Codes for INSTANTLY Better Designs]]: Nested corners need a smaller inner radius (outer radius minus the gap), pills need no correction, and Figma's iOS corner smoothing at maximum makes corners subtly rounder.
- [[sources/eMMiLeo_UGI-the-4-levels-of-landing-page-ui-ux-design|The 4 Levels of Landing Page UI/UX Design]]: Sharp-edged images next to rounded buttons create a disconnect on the level one page.
- [[sources/ek-the-magic-of-clip-path-the-magic-of-clip-path|The Magic of Clip Path]]: clip-path clips an element to a shape: circle(50% at 50% 50%), ellipse, polygon, url() for a custom SVG, or an inset rectangle with rounded corners (round 17px on the tabs).
- [[sources/eks-skills-prototype-picker-emilkowalski-skills-skills-prototype-picker-md|emilkowalski/skills: skills/prototype/PICKER.md]]: The container, highlight and items all use border-radius 999px, making a full pill.
- [[sources/pGYLZyBE32o-i-redesigned-google-s-ai-website-from-scratch-complete-transformation|I Redesigned Google's AI Website from SCRATCH (complete transformation)]]: Fully rounded corners are part of the chosen AI visual recipe.
- [[sources/lkKGQVHrXzE-designing-with-claude-code|Designing with Claude Code]]: Match the screenshot frame radius to the product, keep inset screenshots concentric with their container, drop the bottom radius on a cropped screenshot, and try pill buttons site-wide.

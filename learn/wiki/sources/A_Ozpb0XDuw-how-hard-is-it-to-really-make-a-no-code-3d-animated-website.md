# How hard is it to REALLY make a no-code 3D animated website?

## Summary

Kole Jain, with no prior 3D experience, builds a no-code 3D animated website around a logo he designed earlier, emulating a reference site with a spinning logo in the center and a reel of text moving underneath it. He makes the 3D logo in two tools: Spline, an in-browser 3D tool whose UI resembles Figma, and Blender, the free industry-standard tool that is more complex. In both, the workflow is to import an SVG made in Figma, extrude it, add cameras, lights and effects, then export it as a video; Spline only allows that export on paid plans. He then designs the page in Figma, adds a second section with a 3D gem, and builds the final site in Wix Studio without code. For a design system, the lessons are that a 3D hero asset can carry a marketing page on its own, that lighting has to be checked while the asset rotates, and that the choice of 3D tool trades ease against cost and finish [inferred].

## Key Ideas

- A 3D animated website can be made without code and with free tools.
- 3D on a website impresses people so much that the rest of the page needs less design work.
- Websites with 3D elements are winning awards and clients, which is why the video tries one.
- The 3D workflow is the same in both tools: import an SVG from Figma, extrude it, add cameras, lights and effects, and export.
- Spline is quicker to learn because its in-browser UI looks like Figma, but exporting a video needs a paid plan.
- Blender is free and the industry standard, but more complex; it took longer and many tutorials, yet gave a nicer, metallic result.
- The 3D animation is exported as a video and placed on a no-code site builder (Wix Studio).
- The finished rotating logo shows some dark spots as it turns, which the creator says he would go back and fix with lighting.
- Making both 3D assets took a couple of hours from zero 3D experience; the creator says learning it was worth it and can let a designer offer clients something new.

### Process

1. Pick a reference: Choose a site to emulate; here, a logo spinning in the center with a reel of text moving underneath.
2. Prepare the logo: Use the logo as an SVG file made in Figma.
3. Model in 3D: Import the SVG into Spline or Blender, extrude it, and add cameras, lights, effects and materials (a metallic material in Blender, following a tutorial).
4. Export the animation: Export the animation as a video (Spline requires a paid plan for this; Blender is free) and render the final version.
5. Design the page: Lay out the website in Figma around the 3D asset; here a second section with a 3D gem was added.
6. Build without code: Build the final site in Wix Studio with no code, placing the 3D assets on it.
7. Review and fix: Watch the live result and note fixes, such as adjusting lighting where the rotating logo shows dark spots.

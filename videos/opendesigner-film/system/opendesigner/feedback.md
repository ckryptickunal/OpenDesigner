## F-001 · 2026-10-07 · gap · area platforms
There is no video, film or presentation surface. Platforms are web, ios, android and desktop only, so a 1920x1080 film had to pretend to be a web page, and house standard STD-visual-details-24 (light and dark values for every role) blocked --theme dark-only for a video that plays in one fixed theme.

## F-003 · 2026-10-07 · gap · area typography
The type scale tops out at 95px (display-lg) for a desk viewing distance. A film watched about 390pt wide on a phone needs every type, space and radius token scaled about 2.5x; there is no medium or viewing-distance multiplier, so the mapping was written by hand.

## F-004 · 2026-10-07 · gap · area typography
font.family.text is system-ui, which renders differently per machine. Renders that must look identical everywhere (video, social cards, PDFs) need a shipped typeface; the brand-typeface hook is only listed as a note, never asked as a question when the surface is a render.

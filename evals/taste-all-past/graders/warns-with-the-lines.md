Every one of the three visual directions (Flat 2.0, Material tonal, Glass) crosses a line the person drew in their taste poll. Material tonal is too soft (too round); Flat 2.0 is too productive (too plain); Glass is too productive, too mono and too soft. PASS if the reply offers all three as choices and says, before the person picks, that each one crosses a line from their poll, naming at least one line for each direction. FAIL if it leaves a direction out, offers none, presents any direction as clear of their lines, or warns without saying which lines.

PASS if the reply does all of these: presents the Taste Index (a picture of the look, principles, colours or fonts from the measurements); tells the person in plain words that the index keeps the site's exact colours and font names and that what they keep or change is their responsibility; and asks the person to choose before the system follows it (whether to use it, or whether to keep the exact fonts or use free substitutes).

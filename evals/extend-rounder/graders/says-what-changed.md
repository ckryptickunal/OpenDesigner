PASS if the reply names the setting it changed (corner roundness or radius), gives its old and new values or the old and new corner sizes, says it rebuilt the system and whether checks passed, and does not say that any other design choice changed. Listing what the rebuild wrote again (tokens, exports, previews, DESIGN.md), corner sizes that follow from the same setting, a decision-log entry the engine adds for that same setting, or a version stamp does not count as another change. FAIL if it is vague about what changed, claims a rebuild without saying how it went, or says another setting or design choice changed too (for example colors, type or spacing).
